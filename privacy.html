Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps and games published by Gigacorp ("we", "us") on the Apple App Store and other platforms, as well as this website. Gigacorp is a small games & apps studio based in Sweden.

Some apps have their own privacy policy, which applies instead of this one: Fluffy Fonts.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location through our apps.

Some of our games use AI features. When you use them, the text or other input you provide may be sent to a third-party AI service provider to generate a response. This input is used only to provide the feature, and we do not use it to identify you. Please avoid entering personal information into these features.

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage and crash data to understand how our apps are used and to fix problems. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Third-party services

Our apps may rely on third-party services, such as Apple (App Store, in-app purchases, Game Center) and AI service providers. These services process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not use your data for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data. If you have any questions about data related to you, or want it deleted, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, and delete personal data about you, and to object to its processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
