Fluffy Fonts Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy applies to the Fluffy Fonts app, its keyboard and its App Clip, published by Gigacorp ("we", "us").

We collect nothing

Fluffy Fonts does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no AI features, and it contains no third-party code. The app and keyboard make no network connections, and we have no servers that could receive your data.

What you type

The keyboard turns your letters into styled characters on your device and inserts them into the app you are typing in. It never records, logs or sends what you type. To decide when to capitalize, it looks at the text just before the cursor, and never stores it. Password fields always use the iPhone's own keyboard.

Full Access

All font styles work without Full Access. If you turn Full Access on, the keyboard can read the settings you choose in the app (theme, photo theme and favorite styles) and use haptic feedback. Full Access does not change anything above: nothing you type leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, favorite styles, recently used symbols, the text in the app's typing box and the photo you choose for a keyboard theme are stored only on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app removes them.

The App Clip stores the text you type in it only on your device, and iOS removes it along with the App Clip.

When you tap Copy, the styled text goes to the iPhone clipboard, where your devices may share it through Apple's Universal Clipboard.

Children

Because Fluffy Fonts collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with an updated date.
