Droppy Dot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy applies to the Droppy Dot game, published by Gigacorp ("we", "us").

We collect nothing

Droppy Dot does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it contains no third-party code. The game makes no network connections, and we have no servers that could receive your data.

What stays on your device

Your current level, retry count and best score are stored only on your device. They are included in your device backups like any app data. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Because Droppy Dot collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with an updated date.
