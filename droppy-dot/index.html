Droppy Dot

A puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Draw lines to bounce a falling dot into every coin. 75 levels. No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking.

How to play

Swipe to draw a line. When the dot drops, it bounces off your lines. Catch all the coins on a level to move on to the next one. You can draw up to 20 lines per level.

What is a Perfect?

Clear a level on the first try and you get a Perfect. Clear several in a row for a bigger streak.

How does scoring work?

Your score is the number of retries it took to finish all 75 levels. Fewer is better. Your best score is saved on your device.
